About Me

I'm Obed Ishimwe, a Web Developer and Designer who loves building websites and creating digital experiences. My journey started with curiosity about how the websites I used every day actually worked, and that curiosity grew into a real passion for turning ideas into clean, functional, well-designed products on the web.

My Mission is to build digital experiences that are simple, useful, and genuinely enjoyable to use — solving real problems for the people on the other side of the screen, not just making things that look good.

Education has always mattered deeply to me. From Ordinary Level through my High School Diploma at APADE, and now as I pursue my Bachelor's degree in Information Management at the Adventist University of Central Africa (AUCA), I've carried the same mindset into my work as a developer: stay curious, keep learning, and never stop improving.
